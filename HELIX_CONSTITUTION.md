# The Helix Constitution

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective:** 2026-05-23
**Maintainer:** KamikazeLabs / Helix desk lead
**Status:** live

This is the live constitution governing every paper Helix Site Forge produces. It is short on purpose. Any change to this document ships as a new version with a dated diff; the previous version remains addressable.

---

## I. Addressability

1. Every artifact is addressable: papers, ledger entries, errata, debate transcripts, counter-construction attempts. Once issued, the URL is permanent.
2. Errata ship as new versions. Originals remain addressable and are clearly marked as superseded.

## II. Grounding

3. No quantitative cell ships without a named source row in the appendix.
4. Every figure ties to `(source, transformation, timestamp)`. Unanchored figures do not ship.
5. Every forecast discloses its kill switch — a quantitative condition that, if met, invalidates the thesis.

## III. Reasoning

6. Every paper is an argument graph. Claims are atomic. Justifications are explicit. No orphan claims; no unreachable conclusions.
7. A counter-construction is attempted before every publish. The attempt is published whether it succeeds or fails.
8. Reviewer disagreement is published, not hidden. The published paper includes the disagreement matrix.

## IV. Independence

9. The three AI reviewers hold disjoint priors. Concurrence across disjoint reasoning paths is the signal; concurrence across shared priors is noise.
10. The desk lead's sign-off is mechanical when all gates pass. Discretionary holds are documented in the post-mortem.

## V. Process

11. Twenty gates run on every paper. Failure of any gate blocks publication. There are no partial passes.
12. Cadence is fixed: four quarterly long-thesis papers, one ledger refresh per Friday. The cadence is not a target; it is a contract.

## VI. Honesty

13. Past performance is reported in full, including closed positions that failed. The ledger does not curate.
14. The pipeline measures itself. Telemetry on gate-fail rates, reviewer disagreement, and debate outcomes is available on request.
15. If this constitution is changed, the change is published with a dated diff. The current version is always the live version.
