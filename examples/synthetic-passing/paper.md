## One-page summary

PRISM Diagnostics combines a growing installed base of molecular diagnostics platforms (18,200 instruments at FY25) with high-margin recurring consumables ($760M FY25, 88% gross margin). Its next-generation point-of-care platform (Quantum-2, launching Q3 FY26) adds an addressable installed-base growth vector worth ~4,000 incremental placements by FY28.

**Entry:** below $110 (current $112.40).
**Exit:** $158 (FY28 EPS $5.65 × 28.0×) or kill switch.
**Kill switch (quantitative):** paper invalidates if any of:
- consumables revenue per installed platform falls below $40K in any rolling 4-quarter window (FY25 baseline $42K)
- installed-base unit growth falls below 8% YoY for two consecutive quarters
- gross margin contracts more than 250bp from FY25 (88.0%) at constant mix
- multiple compresses below 22× FY28 EPS at any point in the 24-month review window

**Horizon:** 36 months. **Expected return (base):** ~41% over 3 years; IRR ~12.1%.

## Business overview

PRSM sells molecular-diagnostics instruments (~22% of revenue) and the proprietary consumables that run on them (~64%). Reference-lab services and software are the remaining 14%. Revenue is recurring: ~88% comes from installed-base activity (consumables, service, software) rather than new instrument sales.

## Economic engine

ROIC averaged 25.3% over FY20–FY25 (`row_roic_5yr`), comfortably above the 21.0% top-decile peer threshold (`row_peer_roic`). The economic engine has three layers: (a) instrument placements at modest gross margin (28%) serve as customer acquisition; (b) consumables at 88% gross margin compound on the installed base; (c) menu expansion drives revenue per platform without requiring incremental placements. Per-platform consumables revenue has grown from $34K (FY20) to $42K (FY25), 4.3% CAGR, driven by 47 new assays approved across the period.

## Durability

Switching costs are material. Each platform must be re-validated by the customer lab before a competitor consumable can be substituted — a 9–12 month process. ISO 15189 accreditation and CLIA compliance add procedural friction. PRSM's installed base has had less than 2% annual platform retirement (`row_retirement_rate`), and competitor displacement attempts in the period FY20–FY25 succeeded on fewer than 1% of platforms (`row_displacement_rate`).

## Growth runway

Quantum-2 is a smaller-footprint point-of-care platform launching Q3 FY26 at a $48K list price (~30% below the current flagship). It opens decentralized testing sites — outpatient surgery centers, urgent care, regional hospitals — that the current platform cannot economically serve. Management has guided 4,000 cumulative placements by FY28 (`row_q2_guidance`); we model 3,500 in our base case, 4,500 in bull. At the company's historical consumables ramp curve, that translates to incremental revenue of $115–155M by FY28.

## Management and capital allocation

CEO has led PRSM since 2019. Capital allocation FY20–FY25: 58% R&D + capex (instrument and assay development), 22% M&A (4 tuck-ins, average 8× EBITDA), 20% buybacks. 3 of 4 acquisitions achieved underwritten synergies within 24 months; the fourth (sequencing chemistries, 2022) was written down 35% in FY24. Track record consistent with peer median.

## Financial model

Base case FY28: revenue $2.08B (CAGR 9.4% from FY25 $1.59B), consumables-to-instrument mix shifts further to 68/22/10, gross margin expands to 70.5% (vs FY25 68.9%) on consumables mix lift, operating margin 28.5%, EPS $5.65. Bull EPS $6.30; bear $4.85.

## Valuation and entry

Current: 19.9× FY26E EPS of $5.65 (sic — see appendix). Sector median for diagnostics platforms at PRSM's growth profile is 26× (range 23–30×, `row_peer_multiples`). We target 28×, ~2× premium for the Quantum-2 optionality. FY28E target price = $5.65 × 28 = $158.

## Risks and counters

**R1.** Quantum-2 launch slips or under-delivers. *Counter:* the kill switch on per-platform consumables triggers if the existing base softens (independent signal); on Quantum-2 specifically, our base case is 12.5% below management guidance, allowing for slip.

**R2.** Pricing pressure from payers compresses consumables margin. *Counter:* the kill switch on gross margin triggers at 250bp of contraction; FY28 EPS at the threshold is $5.40, target compresses to $151, IRR remains ~10%.

**R3.** A major customer (hospital system) shifts to a competing platform. *Counter:* top-10 customer concentration is 22% (`row_top10`); no single customer over 4.5%. R3 is contained.

## Reflexivity

If this view becomes consensus before FY27 results print, the multiple expansion compresses our IRR by ~25%. Entry rule: size down 15% for every 50bp of consensus FY28 EPS upgrade above $5.40. Exit half if Street median reaches $5.50 (97% of base case).
