What We Publish

Two deliverables on a fixed schedule: a long-thesis paper each quarter and a public ledger refreshed every Friday.

Quarterly Long-Thesis Papers

Four papers per year, each shipped on the first Wednesday of the quarter. Each runs roughly forty pages, with a one-page summary on page one so you can judge fit in under two minutes.

Public Ledger

Every position carries its entry date, entry price, exit rule, and current state. The full ledger history is available as a CSV download with no gated archive.

Named-Source Citations

Every quantitative cell in every paper ties to a named source row or filing. The pipeline is designed so unanchored numbers do not ship.

What Happens Before a Paper Ships

Every paper clears two readers before it publishes: one fundamental analyst reviews the thesis and sourcing; a quantitative reviewer checks the model, the numbers, and the data ties. Before the desk lead can sign off, a three-AI concurrence review runs independently. All three must reach agreement; dissent holds the paper. The pipeline runs twelve pre-publish gates on every submission: - Number-tie - Citation resolver - Date consistency - Ticker validity - Chart render - Font embedding - Brand check - TOC parity - Page budget - Disclaimer - Spell and grammar - AI triple-review concurrence Per the editorial policy, a paper that fails any gate does not ship. Partial passes are not a category.
