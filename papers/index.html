What We Publish

Two deliverables on a fixed schedule: a long-thesis paper each quarter and a public ledger refreshed every Friday.

Quarterly Long-Thesis Papers

Four papers per year, each shipped on the first Wednesday of the quarter. Each runs roughly forty pages, with a one-page summary on page one so you can judge fit in under two minutes.

Public Ledger

Every position carries its entry date, entry price, exit rule, and current state. The full ledger history is available as a CSV download with no gated archive.

Named-Source Citations

Every quantitative cell in every paper ties to a named source row or filing. The pipeline is designed so unanchored numbers do not ship.

Two Ways to Judge the Desk

Open the public ledger to see every call, its date, and what followed. Read the latest paper to judge the argument before you trust it. One subscription covers both artifacts. Four papers publish each year. Cancel before the next one publishes and the unused quarter refunds pro-rata.
