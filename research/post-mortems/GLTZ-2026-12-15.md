**What actually happened.** Three of the four thesis variables played out directionally as the paper anticipated. The fourth — the magnitude of multiple compression — was the load-bearing step that broke.

1. **Inventory bloat resolved as predicted, via a different mechanism.** Inventory days reached 156 in Q3 FY26 (paper anticipated 142+ persisting). The company cleared by accelerating off-price-channel placement at deeper discounts, recognizing the impact through gross-margin compression (-180bp YoY) rather than direct write-downs. The argument graph's `claim_inventory_bloat` was directionally correct; the *channel* of resolution differed.

2. **SSS continued to decline.** Q3 FY26 SSS was -4.1%, Q4 -3.6%. Paper anticipated persistence. ✓

3. **Demographic shift continued.** Under-25 cohort reached 30% by Q4 FY26. ✓

4. **Multiple compression undershot.** Forward multiple compressed from 28× to 22.1× by 2026-12. The paper's target multiple of 16× was the peer median *at publish time*, but the peer median itself moved up to 22.0× during the holding period as the broader luxury sector re-rated on rate cuts. Compression *to peer median* held; the peer median did not stay where the paper anchored it.

## 7. Counter-construction validation

From the published `counter_attempt.md`:

> Off-price recognition is closer to *net realization* (after rebates and returns), not full wholesale ... If GLTZ's off-price recognition is closer to net realization (analog: ~62% of wholesale), the FY25 reported revenue overstates by $42M, not the implied ~$110M overstatement the paper's body implies.

This was the actually-broken assumption. The counter-construction agent's `recommendation: ship_with_appendix` was the right call structurally, but the desk treated it as a disclosure exercise rather than a thesis-altering input. **The agent's design is validated; the desk's response to it should be tightened.**

## 8. Lessons / what changed

Two desk-level changes from this post-mortem:

1. **Target-price anchoring.** When a thesis depends on multiple compression to a peer-group level, the paper must specify whether the target is a peer-median *level* (snapshot) or *trajectory* (dynamic). The synthetic-short paper conflated the two. Update the mean-reversion-short shape (`helix/shapes/mean-reversion-short.yaml`) to require an explicit trajectory-vs-level declaration in the valuation section.

2. **Counter-construction weighting.** When a `ship_with_appendix` recommendation surfaces a counter that targets a load-bearing edge in the argument graph (weight ≥ 0.7 on its inbound to the conclusion), the desk lead's sign-off must explicitly state how the counter was incorporated into the model — not just disclosed. Update `HELIX_CONSTITUTION.md` §III.7 to require this.

Both changes feed into the next quarter's audit (`research/audits/2027-Q1-...`).
